# Publishing an update

This is the public MIT-licensed distribution. The original export used fresh Git
history and removed selected private metadata. [PUBLIC-EXPORT.json](PUBLIC-EXPORT.json)
records that export; it is not a fresh audit of every later change.

Before another public update, follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the
[privacy checklist](https://github.com/rustybladerunner/jev-skills/blob/main/PUBLICATION.md).
Review the exact files, package, and newly exposed history. Keep the experimental
notice and evidence limits. Don't upload private source history or change another
repository's visibility without the owner's authorization.

The earlier synthetic live routing check is recorded in
[release/RELEASE-STATUS.md](release/RELEASE-STATUS.md). It doesn't mean that every
later revision has had a new live test. Report only the checks that actually ran.
